Dubai has a reputation for being expensive, and it can be — but a large part of what makes the city worth visiting costs nothing. Below is what we actually send people when they ask.

Free things worth doing

1. The Dubai Fountain. Every half hour from six. Stand on the Souk Al Bahar bridge rather than the mall terrace — same view, a fraction of the crowd.

2. Cross the Creek on an abra. One dirham each way, and still the best value in the city.

3. Al Fahidi historical district. Wind towers and courtyard cafés in the old quarter, walkable in an hour.
