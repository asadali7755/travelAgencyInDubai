Dubai has a reputation for being expensive, and it can be — but a large part of what makes the city worth visiting costs nothing. Below is what we actually send people when they ask, grouped so you can pick by budget rather than scrolling through forty entries.

Free things worth doing

1. The Dubai Fountain. Every half hour from six in the evening. Stand on the Souk Al Bahar bridge rather than the mall terrace — same view, a fraction of the crowd.

2. Cross the Creek on an abra. One dirham each way. The five-minute crossing between Deira and Bur Dubai is still the best value in the city.

3. Al Fahidi historical district. Wind towers, coffee museums and courtyard cafés in the old quarter, walkable in an hour.

4. La Mer and Kite Beach. Public beaches with showers and cafés, and the Burj Al Arab in the background at Kite Beach.

Food and drink

This is where Dubai quietly beats its own marketing. You can eat extremely well for twenty dirhams and extremely well for two thousand, and the gap between them is smaller than you would expect.

Friday brunch is the local institution: a set price, several hours, and a much wider spread than the word brunch suggests elsewhere. Book at least a week out for the well-known ones.

What to skip

We would rather say this here than after you have paid for it. A few of the headline attractions are worth exactly one photograph, and the queue costs you an afternoon you could have spent on the water.
