A driver collects you from your hotel in the afternoon and drives out to the red dunes at Al Lahbab. You get around forty minutes of dune bashing, then the camp: camel rides, sandboarding, henna and Arabic coffee, a BBQ buffet under the lights, and tanoura and fire shows before the drive back.

How the evening runs
15:00
Hotel pick-up
Your driver messages on WhatsApp when he arrives. Pick-up times shift by around 30 minutes depending on where you are staying.
16:15
Dune bashing and photo stop
Roughly 40 minutes across the dunes in a 4x4, then a stop on a ridge for sunset photographs. Tell us if anyone gets motion sick and the driver takes it gently.
17:30
Camp: camel ride, sandboarding, henna
Short camel ride, boards for the dune beside camp, henna for anyone who wants it, plus Arabic coffee and dates.
19:00
BBQ dinner and live shows
Buffet with grilled meats, salads and dessert, vegetarian and Jain options on request. Tanoura and fire show while you eat.
21:00
Drop back at your hotel
Back in Dubai between 21:30 and 22:15 depending on your hotel.
