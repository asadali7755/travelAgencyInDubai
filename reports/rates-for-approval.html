Burj Khalifa is the one place we should not undercut. Emaar publishes the gate price itself, and every agency buys against that same number, so the room underneath is a few dirhams of margin rather than a real discount — and a price below the official gate reads to a customer as either a mistake or a scam. The way to win on Burj Khalifa is to bundle it into a package, which is what Rayna does. Undercutting works where we control the cost: safaris, transfers, city tours, and the packages themselves.

How to send this back

Write a number in the Boss column wherever the proposed rate does not work, and leave it blank where it does. Every approved rate goes into one file and appears everywhere on the site automatically — package pages, tour pages and the trip cost calculator — so there is never a second number to keep in sync.
